# DWEA Capture Protocol — v1 (operator)

One page. No engineer required. Phone or DSLR. Read top to bottom; the steps are the order you do them in.

> Goal: a clean orbit of the scene that Brush 0.3 can solve into a Gaussian splat. The CI quality gate (§5) rejects ≥5% unregistered frames and tells you what to re-shoot.

---

## 0. What you need

- A phone (iPhone 12 or newer, any modern Android with manual exposure lock) **or** a DSLR / mirrorless that can shoot 4K video at 24/30 fps.
- Tripod or gimbal recommended for low-light scenes; handheld is fine in daylight.
- A subject that does **not move** — no people walking through, no swaying foliage in heavy wind.
- ~10–15 minutes on site.

If you can carry the camera all the way around the scene without obstruction, it's a candidate. If half the scene is blocked by a wall or fence, **stop** — splats need full coverage on at least three sides.

---

## 1. Pick the hour-of-day

Lighting is **baked in**. Whatever light you capture is the light the final scene will live in. Pick deliberately.

| Lighting | When | Use this for |
|---|---|---|
| Soft daylight | 10:00–15:00, overcast | safest default, most scenes |
| Golden hour | 30 min before / after sunset | hero / cinematic scenes |
| Blue hour | 20–30 min after sunset | moody / character scenes |
| Hard noon sun | 11:00–14:00, clear sky | only if you want the harsh shadows |

**Rules:**
- One hour, one capture. Do not start in soft light and finish in golden — the splat will fight itself.
- Avoid mixed lighting (half sun / half shadow boundary that moves). Wait for the cloud, or move the capture by 30 minutes.
- For **sibling captures** (same scene at different hours, per Realism Bar §5), capture each one as a separate `sceneId` — different `capturedAt`, same `sceneFamilyId`. Do **not** mix into one shoot.

---

## 2. The orbit

You are walking a circle around the subject, taking the camera up and down as you go. Three rings, evenly spaced.

```
                    ─── ring 3 (high)  ~45° down on subject
                  /
                 /  ─── ring 2 (mid)   eye level
                /   /
               /   /  ─── ring 1 (low) ~knee height, camera tilted up
              /   /   /
            ────────────  subject
```

For each ring:

- **Walk around the subject in a full 360° circle.** A circle, not a horseshoe.
- **Keep the subject roughly centred in frame** the whole way around.
- **Take a photo / hold video** every ~10° — that's roughly **36 frames per ring**, **~110 frames total** for three rings.
- The ring radius (how far out you stand) should be ~1.5–2× the subject's largest dimension. Too close → low overlap; too far → not enough detail.

If the scene is large (interior of a room, a vehicle), do the orbit from inside-out instead: stand in the middle, rotate in place, take a frame every ~10°, then step to a new spot and repeat. The ring concept still applies — just rotate rings around the operator.

---

## 3. Overlap

This is the most common reason a capture gets rejected.

- **Photos:** each frame should overlap the previous frame by **60–80%** of its content. Look at a feature in the centre of frame N — it should still be visible somewhere in frame N+1.
- **Video:** shoot at **24 or 30 fps** and walk slowly enough that **two full seconds of footage covers ~10° of orbit**. Faster than that and motion blur kills SfM.

Rule of thumb: if you cannot point at the same window / chair / tree branch in two consecutive frames, you are walking too fast or stepping too far.

---

## 4. Camera settings

| Setting | Phone | DSLR / mirrorless |
|---|---|---|
| Mode | "Pro" / manual | Manual or Aperture Priority |
| Exposure | **Lock AE** (long-press centre on iPhone, AE-L on Android) | Set once, do not change mid-orbit |
| White balance | **Lock WB** (Pro mode) | Set to daylight / cloudy preset; do not use Auto |
| Focus | Tap subject, **lock focus** | Manual focus, single point |
| ISO | As low as conditions allow (≤800 ideally) | ≤800 |
| Aperture | Phone is fixed; do not zoom | f/5.6–f/8 (deep focus across orbit) |
| Shutter | ≥1/60 s if walking; ≥1/30 s on tripod | same |
| Format | HEIC or JPEG (highest quality), 12 MP+ | JPEG fine; RAW only if you'll convert before upload |
| Video | 4K, 24/30/60 fps | 4K, 24/30 fps; disable in-camera stabilisation if it warps frame edges |

**Do not zoom mid-capture.** Zoom changes focal length and breaks pose estimation. Walk closer or further instead.

**Do not turn on HDR / portrait mode / night mode / "AI scene".** These post-process the image differently per frame and confuse SfM.

## 5. Submit the capture

You upload one folder. The folder must contain:

```
captures/<sceneId>/v<n>/
├── frames/                # all stills, OR a single video.mp4
│   ├── 0001.jpg
│   ├── 0002.jpg
│   └── ...
└── capture.json           # sidecar metadata, see §6
```

CI runs the SfM quality gate (`scripts/capture/quality-gate.py`). It returns one of:

- **PASS** → goes to Brush 0.3 training. You're done.
- **FAIL with reason(s)** → operator-grade message telling you exactly what to re-shoot. The most common reasons:

| Reason | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `LOW_REGISTRATION` | <95% of frames could be solved | walk slower, increase overlap to ≥70%, re-orbit |
| `LOW_OVERLAP_REGION` | one section of the orbit had a gap | re-shoot just that arc, denser frames |
| `BLURRY_FRAMES` | mean sharpness score below threshold | tripod or higher shutter; redo the affected ring |
| `MOTION_IN_SCENE` | subject moved between frames | wait until scene is static; clear people |
| `LIGHTING_DRIFT` | exposure changed mid-orbit | lock AE/WB before starting; re-shoot |
| `UNDEREXPOSED` | mean luma below floor | brighter hour, or open aperture / raise ISO |
| `SPARSE_COVERAGE` | not enough frames or ring count <3 | add the missing ring(s), aim for ≥100 frames |
| `SCHEMA_INVALID` | `capture.json` failed schema check | fix the sidecar (see §6) and resubmit |

The gate prints a single-line operator message and a JSON report. Both go in the PR comment.

---

## 6. `capture.json` (sidecar)

One file per capture, alongside `frames/`. UTF-8, JSON, no trailing comma.

```json
{
  "protocolVersion": "1.0",
  "sceneId": "warehouse-foyer",
  "sceneFamilyId": "warehouse",
  "captureVersion": 1,
  "capturedAt": "2026-05-10T15:30:00+02:00",
  "hourOfDay": 15,
  "lighting": "soft-daylight",
  "location": { "lat": 52.5200, "lng": 13.4050 },
  "device": {
    "kind": "phone",
    "make": "Apple",
    "model": "iPhone 15 Pro",
    "lensFocalMm35": 24
  },
  "frames": {
    "kind": "stills",
    "count": 108,
    "format": "jpeg",
    "resolution": [4032, 3024]
  },
  "rings": 3,
  "overlapTarget": 0.7,
  "operator": "alex@dwea.example",
  "notes": "Cloud cover steady; one passerby at frame 47, removed before submit."
}
```

For a video capture, replace `frames` with:

```json
"frames": {
  "kind": "video",
  "path": "video.mp4",
  "fps": 30,
  "durationSec": 42.0,
  "resolution": [3840, 2160]
}
```

JSON Schema is at [`scripts/capture/capture.schema.json`](../../scripts/capture/capture.schema.json) — CI validates against it before running SfM.

**Required fields:** `protocolVersion`, `sceneId`, `captureVersion`, `capturedAt`, `hourOfDay`, `lighting`, `device.kind`, `frames.kind`, `frames.count` (or `frames.fps` + `frames.durationSec` for video), `rings`, `operator`.

---

## 7. Re-shoot decision tree

Got a `FAIL` from the gate. In order:

1. **Read the `reasons` list.** First reason is the dominant one.
2. **`SCHEMA_INVALID` → fix sidecar, resubmit.** No re-shoot needed.
3. **`UNDEREXPOSED` / `LIGHTING_DRIFT` → fully re-shoot.** Pick a different hour-of-day or lock exposure.
4. **`MOTION_IN_SCENE` → fully re-shoot** at a quieter time.
5. **`LOW_OVERLAP_REGION` / `BLURRY_FRAMES` → partial re-shoot.** Re-do only the affected ring or arc; merge into the same `captureVersion` only if frames < 250 total.
6. **`LOW_REGISTRATION` / `SPARSE_COVERAGE` → fully re-shoot** with more frames and slower walk.
7. **`captureVersion` bumps each re-shoot.** v1 → v2 → v3. Old versions stay archived; CI always grades the latest.

If the same scene fails twice with the same reason, file an issue tagged `capture-protocol` so [VisualResearcher](/DWEA/agents/visualresearcher) can review whether the protocol or the scene is the problem.

---

## 8. Quick checklist (print this)

- [ ] One hour-of-day, locked
- [ ] Tripod or gimbal if low light, handheld OK in daylight
- [ ] AE / WB / focus locked, no zoom, no HDR / portrait / night mode
- [ ] Three elevation rings (low / mid / high), full 360° each
- [ ] ~36 frames per ring → ~110 frames total, or 30–60 s of 4K video
- [ ] 60–80% overlap between consecutive frames
- [ ] No moving subjects in the scene
- [ ] `capture.json` filled in and validates
- [ ] Uploaded to `captures/<sceneId>/v<n>/`
- [ ] Gate passed → done. Failed → read reason, re-shoot per §7.
